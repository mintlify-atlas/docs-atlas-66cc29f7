## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your files">
    Click **SELECT FILES** in the app to load images and/or videos you want to upscale. QualityScaler shows a preview of each file with its resolution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an AI model">
    Pick the AI model that best fits your content — LVA for live-action video, RealESR for animation, BSRGAN for photos, or IRCNN for noise reduction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure settings">
    Set your input/output resolution scale, GPU, multithreading, blending level, and output format.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start upscaling">
    Click **UPSCALE** and QualityScaler processes your files. Progress is shown in real time, and video upscaling supports stop and resume.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  QualityScaler requires a DirectX 12 compatible GPU with at least 4 GB of VRAM and Windows 10 or Windows 11. See [System Requirements](/reference/system-requirements) for the full list.
</Note>
